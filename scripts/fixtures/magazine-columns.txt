LEMON DRIZZLE CAKE
A British classic - zesty, sticky and gone in a day.
Serves 10 Prep 20 mins Cook 45 mins
225g unsalted butter, softened Heat the oven to 180C / 160C fan. Butter and line a 2lb loaf tin.
225g caster sugar Beat the butter and caster sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
4 eggs Fold in the flour, baking powder and lemon zest. Spoon into the tin and bake 40-45 minutes.
225g self-raising flour Mix the icing sugar with the lemon juice. Poke holes in the warm cake.
1 lemon, zest and juice Cool in the tin before turning out.
1 tsp baking powder
100g icing sugar
