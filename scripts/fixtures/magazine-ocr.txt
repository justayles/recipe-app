LEMON DRIZZLE CAKE
A British classic - zesty, sticky and gone in a day.
Serves 10
Prep 20 mins Cook 45 mins

INGREDIENTS
225g unsalted butter, softened, plus extra for the tin
225g caster sugar
4 eggs
225g self-raising flour
1 lemon, zest and juice
1 tsp baking powder
100g icing sugar

METHOD
1. Heat the oven to 180C / 160C fan. Butter and line a 2lb loaf tin.
2. Beat the butter and caster sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
3. Fold in the flour, baking powder and lemon zest. Spoon into the tin and bake 40-45 minutes.
4. Mix the icing sugar with the lemon juice. Poke holes in the warm cake and pour over the drizzle.
5. Cool in the tin before turning out.

TIPS
Even better the next day with a cup of tea.
